Savings Calculation Methodology — Multi-Baseline Framework

Strategic savings for October 2026 are captured against the most defensible baseline per category, consistent with prior months:

  • Direct Material – Building Cluster: Avg. Market Rate vs ARL Purchase Avg. Price (ACCL, AIL/MSIL, ACL-Cotton) and Budget vs Actual COGS (APFIL PP yarn, ARMCL).
  • Capex & MRO: Market Rate vs Bidding / Negotiated PO Price (Offer vs Negotiated CS value) across Mechanical, Electrical, Vehicle, Civil and Import Capex.
  • Raw Material – Consumer Cluster: Avg. Market Rate vs Purchase Avg. Price and Bidding/Negotiation (AAFL, ABL, ALEL).
  • Distribution & Logistics: Cost avoidance — fuel price increase absorbed without transport-rate adjustment, and ad-hoc hired vehicles converted to monthly fixed rental.
  • Packaging Development: Last Purchase Price vs Current (LPO) — batch cards, foil, PET bottles, labels, cement bags.
  • Raw Material – Paddy AEL: Market Average / Target Rate vs Lowest / Actual Rate (HRML, FAL).
  • Indirect Procurement – Consumer Electronic: Dealer-rate difference and principal promotional/margin support (ACEL).
